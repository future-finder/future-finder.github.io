## Beyond memorizing templates

The purpose of learning data structures is not to collect implementations. It is to understand which operations a representation makes cheap, and what trade-off it introduces.

## A working checklist

- Identify the required operations.
- State the invariant.
- Choose a representation.
- Prove correctness before optimizing.
