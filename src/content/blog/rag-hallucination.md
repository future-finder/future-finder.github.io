## Problem framing

This demo article distinguishes retrieval errors, evidence-use errors and unsupported generation. It contains no claimed experimental results.

## Reading questions

1. Was relevant evidence retrieved?
2. Did the model use that evidence correctly?
3. Is every important claim supported?

> A useful taxonomy should help diagnose a system, not only rename its failures.
