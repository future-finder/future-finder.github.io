## Reading with a question

Instead of copying every section, a reading note can be organized around four questions: what problem is being solved, what assumption is made, what evidence is presented, and what remains uncertain?

## Evidence table

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Claim | What does the paper say? |
| Method | How is it tested? |
| Evidence | Does the result support the claim? |
| Boundary | Where might it fail? |
